TuneScript Feature Descriptions

1. AI-Powered Transcription:
   Our advanced AI technology accurately transcribes piano music from audio files or YouTube videos into sheet music and MIDI.

2. Sheet Music Generation:
   Automatically create beautiful, printable sheet music from your transcriptions.

3. MIDI Export:
   Download MIDI files of your transcriptions for use in digital audio workstations or other music software.

4. Transcription Management:
   Easily organize, edit, and delete your transcriptions through an intuitive user interface.

5. Smart Search:
   Find transcriptions quickly using our powerful search feature. Filter by title, composer, genre, and more.

6. Rating System:
   Rate transcriptions and see average ratings from other users to find the best quality content.

7. Favorites:
   Save your favorite transcriptions for quick access and create personalized collections.

8. Public and Private Transcriptions:
   Choose to keep your transcriptions private or share them with the TuneScript community.

9. User Profiles:
   Customize your profile, showcase your transcriptions, and connect with other music enthusiasts.

10. Premium Features:
    - Unlimited transcriptions
    - Priority processing for faster results
    - Advanced editing tools for fine-tuning transcriptions
    - High-quality sheet music exports with customizable layouts

11. YouTube Integration:
    Transcribe music directly from YouTube videos by simply pasting the URL.

12. Mobile Responsiveness:
    Access TuneScript on any device with our responsive design, perfect for on-the-go musicians.

13. Community Feedback:
    Provide and receive feedback on transcriptions to help improve accuracy and quality.

14. Learning Tools:
    Use practice mode to learn pieces at your own pace, with features like tempo adjustment and loop sections.

15. Analytics Dashboard:
    Track your progress, see your most-played pieces, and get insights into your music learning journey.
