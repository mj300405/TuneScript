TuneScript Application Guide

Welcome to TuneScript, your ultimate platform for piano transcription and music learning!

Getting Started:
1. Create an account or log in to your existing account.
2. Navigate to the dashboard to see an overview of your transcriptions and activities.

Uploading a Transcription:
1. Click on the "Upload" button in the navigation bar.
2. Choose an audio file (MP3 or WAV) from your device or provide a YouTube URL.
3. Fill in the details like title, composer, and genre.
4. Select whether you want the transcription to be public or private.
5. Click "Create Transcription" to start the process.

Viewing and Managing Transcriptions:
1. Go to "My Transcriptions" to see all your uploaded transcriptions.
2. Click on a transcription to view details, including the sheet music and MIDI file.
3. Use the edit button to update transcription details.
4. Use the delete button to remove a transcription.

Searching for Transcriptions:
1. Use the search bar to find transcriptions by title, composer, or genre.
2. Apply filters to refine your search results.

Rating and Favoriting:
1. Rate transcriptions on a scale of 1 to 5 stars.
2. Add transcriptions to your favorites for easy access.

Premium Features:
1. Upgrade to a premium account for unlimited transcriptions and advanced features.
2. Premium users can access high-quality sheet music exports and advanced editing tools.

Need Help?
If you have any questions or issues, please refer to our FAQ section or contact our support team.
